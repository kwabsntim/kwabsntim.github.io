## The beginnings

My first meet up with theo(@babyface) was that same evening.. because he said he was cooking up something for the fair.I met him and he showed me a demo of an app he had built and named  "sentrylink", i dont know why he choose that name and how we also used it as the team name,but i guess it just happened.That evening we made some discussions with another team member,arthur moses,and then we decided on what to do next. this was not really any big deal. just three tech people sharing ideas on what something could be like.

## preps
this team above had just two weeks to prepare for the innovation fair. i would say we had some things implemented already,like our app idea, but then it needed some finishing and theo was working on that. ben,judah and then moses was working on the presentation document(pptx) and i was working on the ussd aspect of the software. i might say the brainstorming in the sentrylink group chat was very nice lol! theo was always using the middle finger emoji(not insulting but for weird reasons,lol), but then the understanding was there and we all agreed on the best thing for the team tho.lt was like we knew each other already chaleee!
## go hard or go home 
haha!,go hard or go home. this was a thing we said whiles preparing for the competition and i would say it helped us very much. it helped Muka cram a presentation that felt more like a TED talk, it made the rest of the team stay up at night,even the point where we lost fate "we go hard or go home". at this point, i want to tell you why we even adopted this mentality. 

the innovation fair starts from the semi-finals, and it's a two-day program. the teams that made it to the semi-finals were selected from a pool of teams, so you should now understand that we had fought hard to even get to this stage. and all of this had been done by theo way before we even joined the team.

the teams that were kicked out at the semi-final stage went home with nothing. we didn't want to go home with nothing, so we had to go hard or go home lol! and i guess now you know.
